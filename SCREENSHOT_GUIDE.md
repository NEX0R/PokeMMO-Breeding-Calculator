# Screenshot Guide for OCR

To ensure the best OCR results when importing your Pokémon, follow these guidelines:

## How to Take Screenshots in PokeMMO

### Required Information
The screenshot must include:
- ✅ Pokémon name
- ✅ Gender symbol (♂/♀)
- ✅ All 6 IV values (HP, Attack, Defense, Sp. Attack, Sp. Defense, Speed)

### Best Practices

1. **Open the Pokémon Summary Screen**
   - Press the appropriate key in PokeMMO to view Pokémon details
   - Navigate to the "Stats" or "IVs" page

2. **Ensure IVs are Visible**
   - In PokeMMO, you may need to use an IV checker item or NPC
   - Make sure all 6 IV values are displayed on screen
   - IVs should be clearly visible (not hidden behind other UI elements)

3. **Take a Clear Screenshot**
   - Use high resolution (at least 1280x720)
   - Ensure good contrast (avoid dark/dim screenshots)
   - Keep text unobstructed
   - Include the entire stats section

4. **Screenshot Formats**
   The OCR can recognize IVs in these formats:

   **Labeled Format** (Preferred):
   ```
   HP: 31
   Attack: 25
   Defense: 31
   Sp. Attack: 15
   Sp. Defense: 31
   Speed: 31
   ```

   **Slash-Separated Format**:
   ```
   IVs: 31/25/31/15/31/31
   ```

   **Grid/Table Format**:
   ```
   HP    Atk   Def   SpA   SpD   Spe
   31    25    31    15    31    31
   ```

## Screenshot Hotkeys

- **Windows**: `PrtScn` or `Win + Shift + S`
- **macOS**: `Cmd + Shift + 4`
- **Linux**: Depends on distro (usually `PrtScn` or custom)

## Troubleshooting

### If OCR Fails to Extract All IVs:

1. **Manual Edit**: Click on any missing IV (marked with "?") to enter it manually
2. **Retake Screenshot**: Ensure better lighting/contrast
3. **Check Image Quality**: Make sure text is sharp and readable
4. **Verify Format**: Ensure IVs are displayed in one of the supported formats

### Common Issues:

❌ **Screenshot too dark** → Increase PokeMMO brightness
❌ **Text too small** → Use higher resolution
❌ **IVs not visible** → Navigate to correct menu/use IV checker
❌ **Multiple Pokémon in frame** → Focus on one Pokémon at a time
❌ **UI overlapping stats** → Close other windows

## Example of a Good Screenshot

A good screenshot should show:
- Clear, readable text
- All 6 IV values
- Pokémon name at the top
- Gender symbol visible
- No UI elements blocking the stats

## After Upload

1. Review the extracted data in the preview
2. Verify all 6 IVs were extracted correctly
3. Click any "?" values to manually enter missing IVs
4. Select the nature from the dropdown
5. Click "Save All Pokémon"

---

**Tip**: You can upload multiple screenshots at once! The app will process them in batch and show all results for review before saving.
